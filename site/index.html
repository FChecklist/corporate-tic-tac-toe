Corporate Tic Tac Toe

A knockout tournament of the game everyone knows. Your opponent is masked. Three boards a match. Points decide. One champion.

How it playsTell me when it opens

How a match works

1

Meet a masked opponent

A random draw pairs you with someone you cannot see. Nobody can choose or guess a pairing.

2

Play three boards

A 3x3 board, then a 4x4, then a 5x5. Each board is played until it is full.

3

Runs score points

An unbroken line of your marks scores. Longer lines and diagonals score more.

4

Most points wins

The winner goes through to the next round. The last player standing is the champion.

Why companies like it

Everyone knows it

No rules to explain. The scoring card is on screen.

Masked and fair

Opponents are anonymous. A random draw decides who plays X, with a bonus for the second player.

Phone to phone

Moves go straight between the two devices. Our server only introduces them.

Clear winners

A strict 1st to 5th and a top-ten list. No ties.

Quick matches

A match takes about three to seven minutes.

Your brand

Your colours and logo on the arena and the winners' screen.

Clear winners, always

1st to 5th, always

Every game ends with a clear first, second, third, fourth and fifth place.

A top-ten list

Places six to ten are listed too.

No ties

If players are level, the next measure decides. The last measure is a random draw anyone can re-check.

Told in one line

The results say how any tie was decided.

Questions

How many can play?

The first tournaments are for up to 1,000 players. Bigger fields open later.

Do players need an app?

No. It runs in the browser on a phone or a laptop.

Is it fair?

Who moves first is a random draw. The second player gets a few bonus points to even it out. The score is re-computed from signed moves, so it cannot be changed.

Can players see each other's names?

No. Opponents are masked. Only the champion and runner-up are named to the organizer for prizes.
