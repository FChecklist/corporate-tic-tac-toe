For organizers

How an organizer runs Corporate Tic Tac Toe.

Seven steps

1

Create the game

Name it. Pick the date, time and size.

2

Set it up

Choose the settings, the prizes and your brand colour and logo.

3

Publish it free

Publishing is free. You get one link.

4

Pay before it starts

A game begins only when it is paid for.

5

Share the link

Send it in an e-mail, a chat or on a screen. No app to install.

6

Watch it run

Your console only watches. The game runs on the players' own devices.

7

Get the report

A short report arrives by e-mail after the game.

Your team

Organizer

Creates and pays. Sees real names only for prize hand-over.

Hosts

Up to five people who can run the game with you.

Co-hosts

Watch and make announcements. Cannot change the game.

AI helper

A free AI can help you set up and read the game. It can never pay, publish or delete.

Privacy in one line

Players' personal details are deleted within 24 hours after the game. We never e-mail players.
