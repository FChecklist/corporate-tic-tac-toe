Refund policy

Last updated 12 October 2026.

No refunds

We do not give refunds for a paid game.

Credit instead

If an organizer cancels a paid game before it starts, the payment becomes credit for the organizer's account. The credit is valid for 365 days from the date of the original payment and can pay for another game.

A game that did not start

If a published game is not paid for, it does not begin and nothing is charged. If a payment arrives after a game was cancelled or closed, it is kept as credit.

Questions

Write to rajatsiroffice@gmail.com with the game name or the invoice number.
