Guide for organizers

Ten short steps. You can leave and come back.

Try a free game with 10 peopleLog in
Before the game

Set up

Date, time and size

Pick the day and time and how many players. A free demo game is for up to 10 people.

Move clock

Every move has a clock of 10, 20, 30 or 60 seconds. When it runs out, the software plays a square for the player. Two clock-outs in a row lose the board.

Tournament shape

One against one for the whole company, or team against team with teams of 3 or 5.

Share the link

Send one link to the people you invite. Players need only a phone and a name.

On the day

Run the game

Start

You press Start when everyone is in. A late payment opens the game.

Pause

You can pause. While the game is paused the clocks wait.

Big screen

Show the bracket on a projector or a shared video call.

If someone drops

They open the link again and see the board as it is.

Afterwards

Winners and report

The software names the winners and the order has no ties. You get a report by e-mail a few minutes after the game ends. Player details are erased within 24 hours.
