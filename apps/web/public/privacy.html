Privacy notice

Version 2026-10-15. What Corporate Tic Tac Toe keeps, why, for how long and who sees it. This page stands on its own. It is available in Hindi on request: write to hello@corporatetambola.com.

Who is responsible

  • The organizer who invites you decides why your name is used for the tournament.
  • IT Services by Rajat Agarwal provides the software and handles your details for the organizer.
  • For the organizer’s own account, sign-in and billing, we decide.

What we collect, and why

  • From a player: a display name, to show you on the bracket and the results. Nothing else is asked: no mobile number and no e-mail address.
  • A random id of your phone, kept only as a scrambled code, so that one place stays on one phone.
  • Your moves (square and time) under a player code, so the match can be replayed. Who invited you, so the organizer’s limits work. Your two ticks (this notice and your age).
  • If the organizer puts your mobile number in an invitation list, it is used only to send your personal link.
  • From the organizer and Hosts: name, e-mail, mobile number, company name and billing details, to run the account, send mail and issue invoices.
  • From visitors of the public pages: a random visitor id (one cookie, kept one year), a shortened network address, city and the pages seen.
  • Game pages are never tracked. With Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track we only count the visit.
  • From every page: speed and error reports (page, device type, country, what went wrong; no name, no network address).
  • From a request that is refused or fails (a wrong key, a failed payment): a security record with the time, country, browser and only the first part of the network address.

What we do not do

  • We never e-mail players.
  • We do not sell personal data and we run no advertising trackers.
  • We do not show players’ names to anyone outside the tournament.
  • The organizer’s AI assistant never receives a player’s name. We do not use players’ details to train an AI.

How long we keep it

  • A player’s name, seat, phone code, moves, invitation record and requests are deleted within 24 hours after the tournament ends. Our job runs at 23 hours.
  • Names are sealed (AES-256) when you join. From the start, the names on the bracket are also held as plain text so every phone can show them; they go with the rest.
  • The results are kept for 365 days as seat numbers and player codes, without names.
  • The organizer’s details and the tournament settings are kept until 365 days after the tournament, or until the organizer closes the account.
  • We keep billing data for audit and tax reasons.
  • The security record of a tournament is kept 365 days. Its personal part for players is destroyed at the same 23 hours as the names.
  • The security record of a refused or failed request is kept 90 days.
  • Visits to the public pages: 365 days, then only anonymous daily totals. Speed and error reports: 90 days. The log of mail sent (address and subject): 3 days.

Who receives your details

  • Cloudflare: hosts the website and the database. The website runs on Cloudflare’s global network; the database is in Cloudflare’s Asia-Pacific region.
  • Resend: sends e-mail to organizers and Hosts.
  • Razorpay: takes the organizer’s payment. We never see or store card details.
  • The IT Services payment service (itservices-rajat-agarwal.pages.dev): records each payment for the organizer’s invoice and our accounts.
  • GitHub: runs our scheduled jobs and holds a private copy of the payment records. It never holds players’ details.
  • Google: only if an organizer chooses “Continue with Google” to sign in.
  • The full list is on the sub-processors page.

Your rights

  • From the game page a player can leave at any time, ask what we hold, or ask us to correct or delete it. We answer within 7 days.
  • A player can also name another person who may use these rights for them.
  • Leaving the tournament withdraws your consent at once.
  • To name another person you can also write to hello@corporatetambola.com with their name and how to reach them.
  • You may complain to our Grievance Officer, and to the Data Protection Board of India.

Adults only

  • This game is for adults. A player ticks that they are 18 or older before joining. We do not knowingly let anyone under 18 take part.

Cookies and what your device keeps

  • No advertising cookies and no trackers. The only cookie is a first-party visitor id on the public pages, kept one year.
  • Everything your device keeps is listed under What your device keeps.
