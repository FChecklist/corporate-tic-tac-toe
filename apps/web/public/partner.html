Questions people ask

Who can join?

Anyone with a mobile number. You do not have to be a customer, a business or an organizer. Your friends, family and colleagues can join too.

What exactly do I earn?

50% of the software price of a company's first paid pack bought through your link, and 20% of every later pack that company buys within 12 months of its first purchase, even if it does not use your link again. For example a pack for 100 people is ₹2,399: you earn ₹1,199.50 on the first purchase and ₹479.80 on each repeat.

When and how do I get paid?

Commission is credited to your page when the company's payment is confirmed. We pay it to your bank account (or UPI in India) once the payment has settled, and we will ask for your details when your first commission is ready. Tax may be deducted at source where the law requires it.

How long does my link count?

If someone opens your link and buys within 30 days, the sale is yours, even if they come back later on the same phone or laptop. From then on that company is yours for 12 months: its repeat purchases earn you 20%.

Can I buy through my own link?

No. Purchases made with your own mobile number do not earn commission.

Can I see who bought?

No, and nobody sees your details either. You see the date, the pack and your commission. We keep buyers' information protected.

I signed out, or I am on a new phone. What now?

Press “Sign in with your e-mail”. We e-mail you a 6-digit code and a Sign me in button. Use either one. There is no password and no key to remember.

Can I also set up games for Organizers?

Yes. Turn on Help Desk with the same account. You prepare the game, the Organizer publishes it with one tap, and every paid game you prepared earns you the same commission.

Is there a catch?

No fee, no minimum, no contract. A sale stays as earned even if the buyer cancels the game, because we do not give refunds: the payment becomes credit for the buyer’s next game, and using credit earns no new commission.
