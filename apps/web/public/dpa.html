Data processing terms

For organizers. One page.

RolesWhat we processSecurityDeletionHelping youSub-processorsWhen it ends

Roles

  • You are in charge of the players’ details (the controller, or business, or data fiduciary in India). We process them for you (the processor).
  • We act only on your instructions: run your game and send you the report.

What we process

  • Names of players, mobile numbers only if you choose to ask for them, and, if you switch invitations on, who invited whom and what kind of guest.
  • The results, as seat numbers and player codes without names.

Security

  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Names and numbers are also sealed (AES-256). From the start of a tournament, the names on the bracket are also held in plain text until deleted.
  • Players never see each other’s data.
  • Access to your game is limited to you, your co-hosts and your AI assistant, as you choose.

Deletion

  • All player data is deleted within 24 hours after the game ends.
  • A game that did not start (nobody paid) is treated the same way: its player data is deleted 23 hours after it was meant to end.
  • Its people lists, waiting requests and personal links go too. Its settings stay for 30 days so you can reuse them.
  • The results (seat numbers and player codes, no names) stay 365 days, then are deleted automatically.
  • At the end of day 365 everything else about the game is deleted.
  • We keep billing data for audit and tax reasons.
  • You can delete a game’s player data any time from your console.

Helping you

  • We help you answer a player’s request to see, correct or delete their data, or to name another person who may use these rights. Use your console, or write to us.
  • If we learn of a breach that affects your game, we tell you without undue delay.

Sub-processors

  • We use the sub-processors on our list. Changes are shown there.
  • Data may be processed in other countries by these providers, under their own safeguards.

When it ends

  • When your account or the game ends, the deletion rules above apply.
  • On request we give you the information that shows we follow these terms.
