Fair play

Fair, and easy to check

Who plays whom is fixed before anyone joins, and the same rules run on every phone. Nobody can change the draw or the bracket. There is one exception, and it is shown on screen: when boards must be settled, a person decides.

Good to know

Plain answers

Is there prize money?

No. Players never pay and never stake money. Corporate Tic Tac Toe is software only: we never hold or pay prize money. Any gift is the organizer's own, outside the product.

Who decides the winners?

The software only. The phones apply the same rules and our server only stores and passes on the moves. When boards must be settled, a person picks the winner of that one board, and it is shown on screen.

Is it tested for a big crowd?

A computer rehearsal with 1,000 simulated players finished. A live event of that size has not been run yet.

What does it cost?

You pay once per game, before it starts. See the price list, the terms and the privacy notice.
