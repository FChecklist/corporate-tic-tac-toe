Questions

Good to know

How do I set up a game?
Easiest

Option 1: use the free AI assistant

Your own AI assistant builds a draft for you from your own work link. You check it, then publish and pay.

Option 2: set it up yourself

Work in the organizer console. Pick the time, the size and the format.

Do players pay or install anything?

No. Players never pay and there is no app to download. They open your link and type a display name. Nothing else is asked.

Is there any prize money?

No. Players never pay and never stake money. There are no cash prizes and no betting. Any gift is supplied by the organizer, outside the product. Corporate Tic Tac Toe is software only: we never hold or pay prize money. It is for adults (18+), at company events.

Who decides the winners?

The software only. The bracket, the clocks and every result follow one sealed random seed and the same rules on every player’s phone; our server only stores and passes on the moves. No person, including the organizer and us, can change a draw or a result. Cheating is very hard.

How does the game play?

It is a plain 3 by 3 game. X moves first, and three of your marks in a row, in a column or on a diagonal win. A drawn game is replayed at once with the marks swapped, up to 3 games. If all three are drawn, fate decides, so every match has a winner. Every move has a 20-second clock. When it runs out, the software plays a square for the player. Two clock-outs in a row lose the board.

Which formats are there?

A one against one knockout bracket for a crowd, up to the size of your plan (tested with 1,000 players), or team against team with teams of 3 or 5 and one board per seat. There is also a spectator view and a big-screen page.

How is the ranking decided?

There are no ties. The order is the round reached, then total points, then points in the last match, then fate. 1st to 5th are named and a top 10 is listed, with one line saying how each tie was decided.

What if a player loses the connection?

They can open the link again and see the board as it is. The host can pause, and while the game is paused the clocks wait.

How is the organizer verified?

Through the link or the 6-digit code we send to your e-mail. There is no password to remember.

What happens to the data?

Player names (and mobile numbers, if asked) are erased within 24 hours after the game ends. We keep the winners record and the organizer’s details for 365 days, then delete them automatically. A game that never starts is treated the same way. Corporate Tic Tac Toe is designed to follow India’s DPDP Act, the EU GDPR and US state privacy laws such as the CCPA. Read the privacy notice.

Can people in other countries play?

Yes. Anyone with the link can play on their own phone, in any country.
