Rules

  • The board is three rows of three squares. Two players take turns. X moves first, O second. Three of your marks in a row, in a column or on a diagonal win the game.
  • A full board with no three in a row is a draw. A drawn game is replayed at once with the marks swapped, up to three games, so every match has a winner. If all three games are drawn, fate decides.
  • Fate is a number made from the sealed seed, the board and the player. The lower number wins, and anyone can re-check it.
  • Who plays X first comes from a sealed random seed. Its fingerprint is public before anyone joins. The secret is shown after the start, so anyone can check the bracket.
  • Every move has a clock: 10, 20, 30 or 60 seconds, as the organizer chooses. When it runs out, the software plays a square for the player. Two clock-outs in a row lose the board.
  • The host can pause. While paused, no clock runs. A player who loses the connection can open the link again and see the board as it is.
  • In the knockout, players are placed in the bracket by a shuffle from the sealed seed, and winners move up. In a team game, teams have 3 or 5 players, with one board per seat. The team that wins more boards wins the match.
  • The final ranking has no ties: the round reached, then total points, then points in the last match, then fate. The results say in one line how each tie was decided.
  • The software decides every result. Its result is final.
  • The organizer and we have no way to change a draw or a result in the software.
  • Players never pay and never stake money. There are no cash prizes and no betting. Any gift is supplied by the organizer, outside the product.
  • Players are 18 or older, and the game is for company events.
