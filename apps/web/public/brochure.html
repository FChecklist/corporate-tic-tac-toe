Corporate Tic Tac Toe

A tic tac toe tournament for your whole company.

Turn your company event into a knockout of the game everyone knows, on everyone’s own phone. Share one link. No app to download. Easy for you to set up, fun for every player.

Why organizers choose it

1Ready in minutes. Pick the size, the format and the move clock. Start free, change anything until the game begins.
2Everyone plays on their phone. Players open one link and type a display name. A plain 3 by 3 board, three in a row wins.
3Your brand in the game. Add your logo and brand moments for everyone to see.
4Fair for everyone. The software, not a person, draws the bracket from a sealed seed. Its fingerprint is public before anyone joins, and the secret is shown after the start so anyone can check.
5Your team can help. You, up to 5 Hosts and any number of Co-hosts. Only one payment is needed, from any one of you.
6A free AI helper. Paste your AI link into your own AI chat. It builds the draft. You press publish and pay.
7Players pay nothing. Players never stake money. No cash prizes, no betting. Any gift is supplied by you, outside the product. We provide the software and never hold or pay prize money.
8Privacy that is easy to explain. Player names are erased within 24 hours after the game, and also when a game never starts. Designed to follow India’s DPDP Act, the EU GDPR and US state privacy laws such as the CCPA.
