How it works

From setup to winners, in five steps.

One organizer sets it up in a few minutes. Players need only a phone and a link: no app to download.

  1. Create the game

    Sign in with your e-mail. Pick the day, the time and the size. Building and publishing are free. You pay once per game, before it starts. No yearly plan.

  2. Share one link

    Send it in Slack, Teams, Zoom chat, e-mail or WhatsApp, or show it on a big screen.

  3. Players join with a name

    They open the link and type a display name. No app, no account.

  4. The bracket plays itself

    Players play one against one, or in teams of 3 or 5. Every move has a 20-second clock. Winners move up on their own.

  5. Winners and report

    The software names 1st to 5th and lists a top 10. Any gift is yours to supply, outside the product. A report arrives by e-mail.

On the board

Plain rules, the same on every phone.

Three in a row wins

A 3 by 3 board. X moves first. Three of your marks in a row, in a column or on a diagonal win the board.

Every match has a winner

A drawn game is replayed at once with the marks swapped, up to 3 games. If all three are drawn, fate decides. Fate is a number anybody can re-check.

A 20-second clock

Every move has 20 seconds. When it runs out, the software plays a square for the player. Two clock-outs in a row lose the board.

One against one, or teams

A knockout for a crowd, or team against team with teams of 3 or 5 and one board for each seat.

Fair by design

Who plays X first and the bracket come from one sealed random seed. Its fingerprint is public before anyone joins, and the secret is shown after the start. Nobody can change a draw or a result.

The host can pause

While the game is paused no clock runs. A player who loses the connection can open the link again and see the board as it is.
