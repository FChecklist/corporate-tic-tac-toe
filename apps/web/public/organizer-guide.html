Organizer guide

Short notes for a safe, simple game.

Keep it a private game

  • Invite people you know: colleagues, families, customers, partners.
  • Never ask for a fee, a purchase or a donation to take part.
  • Taking part must be voluntary.
  • Players are 18 or older.

Check the law

  • Some countries and states restrict games with prizes. You must check yours.
  • In the United States, prize promotions above a certain value may need registration. Check your state.
  • In India, prizes above INR 10,000 may carry tax deducted at source. Check with your tax adviser.
  • You are responsible for tax on the prizes you give.

Running the game

  • Share one link in Slack, Teams, Zoom chat, e-mail or WhatsApp.
  • Pick the format: a one against one knockout for a crowd, or team against team with teams of 3 or 5.
  • Pick the move clock: 10, 20, 30 or 60 seconds. When it runs out, the software plays a square for the player. Two clock-outs in a row lose the board.
  • Use Pause if you need a break. The clocks wait while the game is paused.
  • Show the big-screen page on a projector or in a shared video call. Spectators can follow the bracket.
  • A player who loses the connection can open the link again and see the board as it is.

Prizes

  • Players never pay and never stake money. There are no cash prizes and no betting.
  • Any gift is supplied by you, the organizer, outside the product.
  • Corporate Tic Tac Toe is software only. We never hold or pay prize money.
  • The first five places are named on the results screen. You hand over any gift yourself.

Your players’ data

  • Tell your players what you do with their details.
  • Names and numbers are deleted within 24 hours after the game. Your report link works for 22 hours, so open it before then.
  • To delete earlier, use your console.

Payment

  • Build and publish for free. The game does not begin until it is paid for.
  • You can pay at any time until the guest data is erased. A late payment starts the game 2 minutes after it is confirmed.
  • To cancel, tap Cancel this game in your console.
  • We do not give refunds. If you cancel a paid game before it starts, your payment stays as credit for your next game for 365 days. Unused credit lapses at the end of day 365.
  • Once the start time has passed, a paid game cannot be cancelled.
  • Want another game like this one? Tap Create a new game like this one. Your credit is offered first on the Pay step.

Someone to set it up for you

  • A Help Desk person can prepare a game in your account. You allow them first, and you can stop them at any time.
  • They can create and change the game. They cannot publish, pay, see your sign-in keys or close your account.
  • When it is ready they ask you to publish. You get an e-mail with a summary and publish with one tap.
  • After you publish, they become a Host of that game. They do not use one of your 5 Host places.
  • Find a helper, or become one: Help Desk.
