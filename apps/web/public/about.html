About Corporate Tic Tac Toe

Corporate Tic Tac Toe is a tic tac toe tournament for company events and teams. The organizer picks a size and a format and shares one link. Players open it on their own phone and type a display name. The software draws the bracket, runs every match on a clock and works out the winners.

It suits a virtual holiday party, an all-hands opener, a new-hire welcome, a customer event, a Friday team break or a family day. Teams can be remote, hybrid or in one room.
