Questions people ask

Who can be a Help Desk person?

Anyone who has joined Refer & earn. Help Desk is a switch on the same account.

What can I do in the Organizer’s account?

Create a game and change its settings and prizes. That is all. You cannot publish, pay, see sign-in keys, change the e-mail or close the account.

What do I see about the guests?

Only what you type yourself. After the Organizer publishes, you are a Host of that game and see what a Host sees.

How do I earn?

Every paid game you prepared earns you Refer & earn commission, at the same rate and with the same rules as any other sale. Buying through your own number does not earn.

What if the Organizer changes their mind?

They stop it with one tap. Your access ends at once. Games they already published stay as they are.
