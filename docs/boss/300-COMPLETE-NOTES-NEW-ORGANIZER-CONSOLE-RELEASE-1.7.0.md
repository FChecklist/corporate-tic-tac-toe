## 3. Step 1 "When are you planning this online team engagement activity?"
Occasion chips + Other (60 chars). Day and date chips + Other date picker (shows day and date). Time chips + Other time. Unpaid game up to 30 days ahead, paid up to 365.

## 4. Step 2 "Who are you creating this game for?"
Three cards: (1) Your people: staff, associates, contract staff and everyone who works with you, in any location. (2) Your people and their families: your people, in any location, any place, and their families too; everyone gets their own ticket and 4 FAMILY PASSES (1 + 4), fixed, no other option; the person who shares a pass approves who joins (section 9). (3) Extended family: your people, vendors, customers and friends of the company, wherever they are, whichever location; people in the game can invite a few more (4 each). Every ticket holder = one participant for size and price.
Size = drop-down of the game's REAL pack sizes, pre-selected, price shown ("Pick the closest").
Two buttons: "System validates for you" (recommended for cards 1-2) and "You validate yourself" (recommended for card 3).
- System validates: criteria "Employee ID" or "Email ID" (one), "Choose which one you want."; green box "No personal details (name, mobile) are taken from you. Only the [email IDs / employee IDs] you add are allowed to participate. The system sends no email to your participants. You share the game link yourself. All participant information is deleted within 24 hours of the game ending."; paste box ("Copy and paste the employee [email] IDs here, one on each line", Excel tip) or upload + "Download the file format"; list check optional (silent clean on Next; viewer for 600+ rows with search, problems only, 50 per page); someone not on the list waits to be let in.
- You validate yourself: no list; everyone waits; organizer/Host/Co-host lets in.
Ticket number is sacred (unique, never reused, every log keyed by it). Name is optional on the ticket. First mobile at game time keeps the ticket.
