## The rule (owner's words, made precise)
- **We do not refund.** If the Organizer or a Host cancels a PAID game before its start time, the amount paid stays with us as **credit** for that Organizer's account. They can use it for a **next game within 365 days**. Unused credit is **consumed (lapses) at the end of the 365th day**. Cancelling an UNPAID game has no money effect. A paid game still cannot be cancelled after its start time (unchanged).
- **Rescheduling is the gentle option**: the console's cancel flow and the Pay/Check steps point to "Change the date" (reschedule keeps the game and its payment, no credit needed). Rescheduling a paid game is allowed until the start time and, after the start time passes without the game starting, until the guest data is erased (see spec 5).
- **Validity**: 365 days counted from the date of the original payment, until the end of that day in India time (23:59:59 IST of day 365). State the exact date to the user everywhere (console, e-mail, pay page).
- **A payment that arrives after a game was cancelled** (the buyer had the payment window open) is not refunded either: it becomes credit the same way, and the buyer's Organizer/Host is told.
- **Partner (Refer & earn) commission** stays as earned on the original payment (the sale stands); using credit is not a new sale and earns no new commission; a top-up paid with Razorpay for a bigger game is a normal payment.

## How credit works
- Table `credit` (new migration 0018): id, org, source payment id, source game id, amount_inr_total (the total paid including GST), remaining_inr, currency, created_ms, expires_ms, status active|used|expired, plus a `credit_use` table (credit id, game id, amount, at_ms). INR only at first (USD payments: same logic in USD).
- **Using credit**: at the Pay step and the pay page, if the account has active credit the page says "You have credit of Rs X (valid until 14 Jun 2027). Use it for this game." Apply oldest-expiring first. If credit >= price: pay with credit, no Razorpay; the game is marked paid ("paid by credit") and starts like any paid game. If credit < price: use all of it and pay only the difference through Razorpay (same pay page/pay link). If credit > price: the remainder stays active with the same expiry. A pay link (anyone can open) also shows the credit-reduced amount only for the Organizer/Host/Co-host who created it (never leak credit to an outside payer: the pay link charges the full price unless the creator applied credit before creating the link).
- Applying credit is idempotent and atomic (no double use, no negative remainder), logged in the audit trail, and refused for a cancelled/expired game.
- **Invoices**: the original GST invoice stands (advance already invoiced). A game paid by credit gets a normal invoice record that says "Paid from credit of invoice <number>" with no new GST on the covered part; a top-up gets a normal invoice for the difference. Flag in docs that the owner's CA should confirm the GST treatment of advances and lapsed credit (no credit note is raised for lapsed credit).
- **Expiry**: the lifecycle tick marks credit expired at expires_ms (no refund, no credit note), records it, and the Organizer sees it in Your account as "Credit that lapsed".
- **E-mails (Organizer and Host only)**: on cancel ("Your game is cancelled. Your payment of Rs X stays as credit until 14 Jun 2027. Use it for your next game."), 30 days before expiry and again 3 days before ("Your credit of Rs X lapses on ..."), on use ("Rs X of credit used for <game>"), on lapse ("Your credit of Rs X lapsed on ..."). One of each per credit, idempotent.
