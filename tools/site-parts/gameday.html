How a game day feels

Easy for you. Fun for your people.

  1. You share one link

    Send it by WhatsApp, e-mail or your intranet, only to the people you invite.

  2. Everyone plays together

    Colleagues play one against one, each on their own phone. Every move has a clock, and the software finds every winner.

  3. Winners are cheered

    You get a report by e-mail. Player details are erased within 24 hours.
